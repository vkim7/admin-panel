# Operations Playbook

Back-office operations guide for running the online English school.

---

## Teacher Utilization

| Utilization | Effect |
|-------------|--------|
| Below 50% | Teachers aren't earning enough, they leave |
| **70-85%** | **Sweet spot.** Busy, earning well, buffer for new students and reschedules |
| Above 90% | No room for growth, no buffer, burnout starts within 3 weeks |

**Hire a new teacher when:** existing teachers consistently hit 80%+ for 2+ weeks, waitlist reaches 5+ students, or a specific time slot is fully booked.

---

## Teacher-to-Student Ratios

- 1-on-1 lessons: 1 teacher handles ~15-20 active students (assuming 2-3 lessons/week per student)
- Group lessons (6 students): 1 teacher can run 4-5 groups = 20-30 students
- Mixed model: plan for ~1 teacher per 15-20 students initially

## Conversion Funnel Benchmarks

| Stage | Target |
|-------|--------|
| Lead → trial lesson booked | 20-40% |
| Trial → first paid package | 25-50% |
| Overall lead → paid | 8-15% |

**Trial lesson structure:** 15-30 min (not full 50 min). Level assessment + mini-lesson + study plan recommendation. Follow up within 2 hours. Offer a first-package discount valid 48 hours.

## Student Churn

- **Typical monthly churn:** 5-10%
- **Good:** below 5%
- **At 5% monthly:** lose ~half your students annually
- **At 10% monthly:** lose ~70% annually

**Top causes:**
1. Perceived lack of progress (students don't see improvement after 2-3 months)
2. Schedule friction (can't book preferred times)
3. Poor teacher match
4. Price sensitivity (71% cite price as top reason)
5. Life changes

**Reactivation sequence for churned students:**
- Day 30: "We miss you" message from their former teacher
- Day 45: Feedback survey
- Day 60: Free welcome-back lesson or 15-20% discount
- Day 90: "Your progress report is waiting"
- Target 5-10% reactivation rate

## Scheduling Operations

### Teacher Absences
- Planned vacation: minimum 2 weeks notice, students notified and offered substitute or reschedule
- Sick day: notify 4+ hours before lesson, substitute system activates
- No-show: warning on first offense, three no-shows in 30 days = review/termination

### Substitute System
- Maintain 3-5 teachers per level/time zone willing to accept short-notice lessons
- If no substitute within 30 minutes: notify student, offer reschedule, credit, or access to lesson recording (for group lessons where the class still ran)
- Student cancellation fees: 24+ hours = free, 6-24 hours = 50%, under 6 hours = 100%

### Student-Teacher Matching (Priority Order)
1. Schedule compatibility (non-negotiable)
2. Level match
3. Learning goals (conversational, IELTS, business, kids)
4. Teaching style preference
5. Personality fit

### Group Class Management
- Minimum viable group: 3 students. Don't run with fewer.
- Maximum: 6 for our model
- Open enrollment 2 weeks before start. Cancel and reassign if fewer than 3 enrolled 48 hours before.
- Students can be moved between groups by admin

## Financial Operations

### Unit Economics Per Lesson

| Component | % of Student Price |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Teacher pay | 40-60% |
| Platform/overhead | 15-25% |
| Marketing/CAC | 10-20% |
| **Net margin** | **10-25%** |

### Pricing Strategy
- Offer 3 tiers (monthly packages: 8/12/16 lessons)
- Bulk discounts of 10-20% for larger packages
- Never discount more than 25%
- Target LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1

### Refund Policy
- 14-day cooling-off period: full refund if no lessons taken
- After that: pro-rated credit for unused lessons (not cash refund)
- No refund for: lessons taken, no-shows, after cooling-off period

### Cash Flow
- Students pay monthly in advance (positive cash flow)
- Teachers paid biweekly
- Maintain 2-3 months of teacher payroll as cash reserve

## Quality Assurance

### By Scale

| Teachers | QA Method |
|----------|-----------|
| 5-10 | Founder observes 1-2 lessons per teacher per month (live or via Zoom recordings) |
| 10-25 | Lead teacher reviews Zoom recordings |
| 25-50 | Dedicated QA person, sample 10% of Zoom recordings, post-lesson ratings |
| 50+ | QA team (1 per 30-40 teachers), mystery students quarterly |

### Student Satisfaction Metrics
- Post-lesson rating: 1-5 stars after every lesson. Flag anything below 3.
- NPS quarterly: target 50+
- Progress perception: monthly check-in asking if students feel they're improving

### Complaint Escalation
1. First complaint: acknowledge within 4 hours, review recording, offer free lesson with different teacher
2. Pattern (2-3 about same teacher): observation + improvement plan (30 days)
3. Persistent: reassign students, teacher on probation
4. Severe (inappropriate behavior): immediate suspension

### When to Fire a Teacher
- Average rating below 3.5/5 over 30 days
- Retention rate 20%+ below school average
- 3+ substantiated complaints in 60 days
- No-show rate above 5%
- Inappropriate behavior: immediate termination

| Students | Staff Needed |
|----------|-------------|
| 0-50 | Founder does everything. Spreadsheets + Telegram groups. |
| 50-150 | Part-time operations coordinator |
| 150-300 | Full-time ops manager + academic coordinator |
| 300+ | Add QA specialist, recruiter, dedicated support |

## What Breaks First (In Scaling Order)

1. **10-20 students:** Manual scheduling unsustainable → need software
2. **20-30:** Teacher quality varies → need standardized QA
3. **30-50:** Can't personally respond to everyone → need systems
4. **50-75:** Billing complexity → need accounting/billing system
5. **75-100:** Manual student-teacher matching fails → need database/algorithm
6. **100+:** Ad-hoc teacher hiring doesn't work → need recruitment pipeline

## Seasonal Demand

| Period | Demand | Strategy |
|--------|--------|----------|
| January | High (New Year) | "New Year, New Language" promotion. Have teachers ready. |
| Feb-April | Steady | Focus on retention |
| May-June | Peak (exams) | Maximum capacity. Reduce teacher vacations. |
| July-August | Low (summer) | Summer intensives. Allow teacher vacations. |
| September-October | High (back-to-school) | Second hiring wave. Market in August. |
| Nov-December | Moderate, declining | Holiday promotions. Gift packages. Prep for January. |

## Early Warning Signals

### Teacher Burnout
- Utilization above 90% for 3+ consecutive weeks
- Increasing cancellation rate
- Declining lesson ratings (4.5 → 4.0 over 2 months)
- Slower responses to messages
- Requests to reduce hours

### Student About to Churn
- Reduced lesson frequency (3/week → 1/week)
- Missed lessons without rescheduling
- Two consecutive low ratings
- Stops responding to check-ins
- Switches to shortest package

### Systemic Quality Drop
- Average ratings trending down across multiple teachers
- Declining trial-to-paid conversion
- Student progress metrics stalling across the board
